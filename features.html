Mobile Office Manager · Dashboard

The day opens on what needs you.

Nine connected modules behind one dashboard: sales, contracts, projects, finance, inventory, equipment, scheduling, dispatch and analytics. You land on open callbacks and tests coming due, not on a menu.

The widgets are there on day one

Open work orders, callbacks, contract profitability and the month's billing are already on the board. You are not building a dashboard out of an empty grid.

Arrange it the way your morning runs

Drag widgets where you want them, or move them from the keyboard. Nothing saves until you say so, and your arrangement comes back on reload.

Personal boards and team boards

Several dashboards, searchable, with the one you built for yourself kept apart from the ones the branch shares.

A chart for the question you are asking

Pick from the chart library, or build a widget of your own when nothing in it answers the question.

Click a bar to see the units behind it

Charts drill into the records they came from, so a month of callbacks turns into the list of units that caused them.

Take the numbers with you

Any chart out as PDF, any widget out as Excel, or shown as a table when the owners' meeting wants rows.

Mobile Office Manager · Dispatch

A dispatch board you can actually drag.

Rebuilt from scratch around the way a callback lands mid-morning. Move a job, stretch its edge, and undo it if you were wrong.

Drag and drop, then undo

Move a callback between technicians and times. The board updates instantly and rolls back on its own if the save fails.

Change a job's length by dragging its edge

With a live time readout while you drag, so a category 1 test does not quietly take the rest of the afternoon.

Double-booking is flagged on the row

You find out at the moment you create the clash, not when the technician calls from the lobby.

Approved time off blocks the slot

Requests carry approvers and a decision trail, and the board respects the answer.

Find the nearest technicians to a job

Assign on where the trucks actually are, with unassigned callbacks sitting beside the board.

Week, day and month

The board shows the working day rather than empty night hours, and a modernization shift that runs past midnight stays one job.

Route and location intelligence sits inside dispatch rather than in a separate app. See GPS Tracker & Route Builder.

Mobile Office Manager · Lists

Every list works the same way.

Tickets, purchase orders, parts, units and the chart of accounts. One layout, learned once.

One list layout across every screen

The pattern is written down as a standard, so each new list behaves like the last one you learned.

A filter box under every column heading

Plus per-screen advanced filters, and the button tells you when they are on.

Group rows, then group the groups

Grouping nests several levels deep, with totals in the footer and money shown in the branch's own currency.

The grid remembers how you left it

Columns, widths, grouping and sort come back the next time you open the screen.

Tick rows, then act on them together

Mass-update tickets straight from the list, with only the actions that user is allowed to run.

Export the whole filtered list

Not just the page you are looking at. The filtered set, out to Excel.

Mobile Office Manager · Records

Every record works the same way too.

Same header, same tabs, same save, whether you opened a unit, a contract or a work order.

One screen for adding, editing, viewing and copying

Instead of four different screens that each behave slightly differently.

The header stays on screen while you scroll

Status chips and headline totals stay visible, so you always know which unit you are looking at.

Step to the next record without going back

Walk the list from inside the record instead of returning to it between each one.

Save sends only what you changed

And you get a prompt before you leave a record with unsaved work on it.

Full change history, down to the line

Field by field, with email history and the customer's signature from the field attached to the same record.

Drag documents onto the record

Certificates, test reports and site photos land on the unit they belong to, and you decide per file who is allowed to see it.

Mobile Office Manager · Getting around

One search box, and a menu that knows who you are.

Branches you can switch without losing your place.

Search every record type from one box

Customers, units, tickets and invoices, keyboard-first, with your recent records there before you have typed anything.

The menu only shows what you can open

Built from your modules and filtered by permission, several levels deep.

Work across several branches at once

Change branch in one tab and the others are told.

Menu wording follows your own terminology

If your team says unit where the last system said asset, the menu says unit.

Collapse the menu to a rail

Give a wide dispatch screen its full width back, with flyout menus so nothing is lost.

Drawers that leave the page in view

Detail slides over the content without covering the menu or the top bar.

Mobile Service

The job gets captured where the job happens.

The field app for iOS and Android, including the parts of the building that swallow the signal.

Genuine offline work

Notes, parts, photos and signatures are captured in the hoistway and the machine room, and sync on their own once the technician is back above ground.

Unit history at the car

Dispatch opens the ticket against the right customer, location and unit, so the technician arrives with the last callback on that unit already attached.

Signature and photos on site

Captured against the work order, so the close-out does not wait on paperwork in a van.

Status the office can see

Technician status controls, with two-way messaging between the route and the dispatch board.

Hours that reach payroll

Time recorded at the unit flows through without being keyed in a second time. It is the change that halved payroll prep at Elevator One.

Nothing gets re-typed

Field data returns into the office record, and billing continues from it the same day.
