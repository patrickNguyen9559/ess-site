Features

Multi Branch and Sites

Run more than one branch, yard or service territory out of one database. Work, technicians, inventory and contracts stay attached to the branch that owns them, while an owner still sees the whole company without switching systems.

Cora AI Assistant

Cora is the AI assistant built into ESS.

Mobile Service FMS

The field app the technician carries. Time, parts, readings, photos and signatures are recorded at the unit and land in the office without anyone keying them a second time — the change that halved payroll prep at Elevator One.

Dispatch and Scheduling

One board for the day's work. Assign, move and re-sequence jobs against the people who are actually available, and the field sees the change without a phone call.

GPS Tracking

See where the trucks are before committing to a callback, so dispatch runs on real positions rather than on the last thing someone said over the radio.

Online Banking Feed

Bank activity comes into ESS rather than being exported and reconciled by hand, so the ledger and the statement stop drifting apart between month ends.

Online Payment

Customers pay the invoice from the same place they read it, and the payment posts against that invoice instead of arriving as a cheque somebody has to match up.

Customer Portal

Property managers and building owners see their own equipment, inspection dates, service history and invoices without calling the office first. It is the view more RFPs are asking for.

Project Management and Job Costing

Modernization and new install run as projects with their own budget. Labour, material and subcontractor cost land against the job as they happen, so margin is visible while there is still time to act on it.

Recurring Maintenance

Contract work generates itself on the cadence the contract sets. Routes, checklists and due dates come from the agreement rather than from a spreadsheet one person maintains.

Progress Billing

Bill a long job in stages against what has actually been completed, instead of waiting for the end of it or guessing at a percentage.

WIP Reporting

Work in progress, costed. What has been spent against what has been billed on every open job — the number that tells you whether the year is going the way you think.

CRM and Proposals

Opportunities, quotes and proposals sit next to the service history they came from, so whoever writes the number can see what that building has already cost to serve.

Accounting

The ledger is part of the platform rather than a separate system to export into. Invoices, payments, payroll input and job cost all resolve to the same books.

Dynamic Dashboard and Reports

Dashboards built around the questions the morning actually starts with, and reports you can change yourself instead of filing a request and waiting.

SMS Notifications

Text the people who need to know: a technician on the way, a job closed, an inspection coming due. Fewer calls in both directions.

Inventory Management

Stock on the shelf and stock on the truck, counted the same way. A part used on a job leaves inventory and lands on the job cost at the same moment.

Equipment Management

Every unit is a record: serial, location, controller, service history, test dates. The building's equipment list is the spine the rest of the system hangs off.

Audit Trail and Edit Logs

Who changed what, and when. Edits are logged rather than overwritten, which is what you need when an inspection or a dispute asks you to show your work.
