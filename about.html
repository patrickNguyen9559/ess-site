We Solve, You Succeed!

Office, field and customer, working on one record.

A service call is not three documents in three systems. Mobile Office Manager, Mobile Service and the Customer Portal read and write the same database, so the record moves through the day instead of being retyped at every handoff. That single idea is the company.

01The office opens the work order in Mobile Office Manager against the customer, the location and the unit it belongs to.
02Dispatch places it in a technician’s day, with route intelligence ordering the stops around drive time and the tests already due.
03Mobile Service shows that technician the unit’s history and the last callback before the panel comes off.
04Hours, parts, photos and the customer’s signature are captured at the unit, offline if the machine room has no signal.
05It lands back on the same record. Payroll reads the hours, inventory reads the parts, billing reads the closed work order.
06The property manager opens the Customer Portal and sees the visit, the next inspection and the invoice without calling anyone.

Six steps, one record. The two places this normally breaks are the handoff from dispatch to the truck, and the handoff from the truck back to billing. Neither one is a handoff here.

What we optimize for

Five decisions that shape everything we ship.

None of them are clever. They come from watching how an independent service contractor really spends a Tuesday.

The unit is the record

Contracts, inspection history, callbacks, parts and photos hang off the individual car, escalator or door, not off a customer note. Ask what happened on that unit in March and there is one answer, not four opinions. It is also how the MCP book stays honest as the route changes hands.

One database, three ways in

The office, the truck and the building owner are not separate systems wired together after the fact. They are three views of the same data, so a signature captured at the unit is already payroll, already inventory, already an invoice.

Offline where the work happens

Hoistways and machine rooms have no signal, so Mobile Service does not ask for one. The technician works the job through to close-out and the sync happens on its own when the phone comes back above ground.

Modular, so you run what you need

Mobile Office Manager is nine connected modules. Start with the part of the business that hurts most, turn on the rest when you are ready, and nothing has to be migrated a second time to get there. Most contractors begin with contracts and dispatch.

Support from people who know the trade

Onboarding, data migration and ESS-U training are run by people who can talk about a category 5 test without a glossary. When you call support, you are not explaining your own industry first, and the answer comes back in the language your dispatcher already uses.
