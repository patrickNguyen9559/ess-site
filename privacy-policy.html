Privacy Policy

How ESS handles information when you use our website, contact our team, or interact with our products and services.

Last updated: September 19, 2026
On this page

1. Overview

Expert Service Solutions, Inc. (“ESS,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, request a demonstration or trial, use our support channels, or interact with ESS products and mobile applications.

Customer production data processed through ESS software may also be governed by a separate customer agreement, data processing agreement, or other contract. If there is a conflict, the applicable signed agreement controls for that customer relationship.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

We may collect information you submit directly, such as your name, company, business email address, phone number, job title, messages, demo or trial requests, support requests, and other information you choose to provide.

Usage and device information

When you use our website or applications, we may receive technical information such as browser or device type, operating system, IP address, pages viewed, referring pages, timestamps, app version, and diagnostic or performance information.

Customer and service data

When ESS provides hosted software or support, we may process data on behalf of customers to deliver, secure, troubleshoot, maintain, and improve the contracted service. The customer remains responsible for determining what information it submits to ESS products and for providing any notices or permissions required by applicable law.

3. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and support ESS websites, software, mobile applications, and services.
  • Respond to inquiries, demo requests, trial requests, and support needs.
  • Authenticate users, protect accounts, detect abuse, and maintain security.
  • Improve product performance, reliability, usability, and customer experience.
  • Communicate service, product, training, or company updates where permitted.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable agreements.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information as a business model. We may disclose information to service providers that help us host, secure, operate, support, analyze, or communicate about our services; to professional advisers; in connection with a business transaction; or when required to comply with law, protect rights, investigate misuse, or address security issues.

Third-party services, integrations, app stores, or websites have their own privacy practices. We encourage you to review those policies before providing information to them.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies, local storage, analytics technologies, or similar tools that are necessary for site functionality or help us understand site performance. Where required, consent choices should be presented before non-essential technologies are enabled.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to provide services, satisfy contractual requirements, resolve disputes, maintain security records, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by data type, product, customer agreement, and applicable law.

7. Security

ESS uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection to certain processing. You may also opt out of non-essential marketing communications using the unsubscribe method provided in the message.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

9. Children’s privacy

ESS business services are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly seek personal information from children through this website.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, practices, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date above indicates when this page was most recently revised.
