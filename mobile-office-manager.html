Smart connections

The office, the hoistway and the customer share one record.

Mobile Office Manager is not a separate program the field app exports to at night. Mobile Service reads and writes the same records: the unit, the contract, the ticket, the hours. The Customer Portal is the same data again, filtered down to what a property manager is allowed to see.

Dispatch and routing sit inside this too. The GPS Tracker & Route Builder works from where the technicians actually are, so the board in the office and the day on the phone stay in step.
